# src/lists/

What the lists of songs and clips share: the filter, its bar, ★ / ✕ and the offer to undo.

- `FilterBar.css`: the look of FilterBar.tsx
- `FilterBar.tsx`: the filter bar above a list
- `list-filter.ts`: the filter both lists share
- `lists.css`: what the lists share: the rows, the tag chip, the small row buttons and warnings
- `StatusButtons.css`: the look of StatusButtons.tsx
- `StatusButtons.tsx`: ★ and ✕ for a song's or a clip's status
- `time-groups.ts`: a list grouped by when its items were made: just now, last 15 minutes, … older
- `UndoBar.css`: the look of UndoBar.tsx
- `UndoBar.tsx`: the offer to undo a change that hid something
- `use-list-filter.ts`: a list's filter kept in the address
- `use-now.ts`: the time now, kept current every minute
