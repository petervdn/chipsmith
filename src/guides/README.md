# src/guides/

The style guides: their page, the editor, and the chips that choose them for a prompt.

- `GuideChipRow.css`: the look of GuideChipRow.tsx
- `GuideChipRow.tsx`: the style guides as a row of chips to choose
- `GuideChips.css`: the look of GuideChips.tsx
- `GuideChips.tsx`: the style guides' chips in a panel under the New page's prompt box, their texts to read
- `GuideEditor.css`: the look of GuideEditor.tsx
- `GuideEditor.tsx`: one style guide opened to edit, or a new one
- `guides.css`: what the guides' parts share: the buttons to make and save a guide
- `GuidesPage.css`: the look of GuidesPage.tsx
- `GuidesPage.tsx`: the style guides, made and edited
- `use-guide-choice.ts`: the style guides chosen for a prompt
- `use-guide-editor.ts`: a style guide's draft, saved or deleted
