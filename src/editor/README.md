# src/editor/

The song editor on an opened song's page: the timeline of its tracks and blocks, edited as a draft and
saved, playing from a cursor, the zoom, and the selected block's clip or track.

- `block-drag.ts`: where a dragged block goes: moved on the grid and between tracks, or trimmed
- `BlockDetails.css`: the look of BlockDetails.tsx
- `BlockDetails.tsx`: the selected block's section, one bar: where it plays, the clip it plays, Show track, Loop it, solo, repeats, duplicate, untrim, remove
- `ClipDetails.css`: the look of ClipDetails.tsx
- `ClipDetails.tsx`: a clip's section: its facts, its .fur, its takes, quick changes; the prompt for takes of it beside them; the note grid under
- `DetailsSection.css`: the look of DetailsSection.tsx
- `DetailsSection.tsx`: a section of the details as a card, or a bar: its kind (block, clip, track) over its title
- `clip-prompt.ts`: the prompt on a clip: its quick asks, how a tap adds one, the most takes
- `ClipPrompt.css`: the look of ClipPrompt.tsx
- `ClipPrompt.tsx`: the prompt on a clip: what to change, how many takes, Send, the jobs on their way
- `ClipTakes.css`: the look of ClipTakes.tsx
- `ClipTakes.tsx`: the takes of a clip as pills: a tap puts one in its place; the one shown renamed or deleted
- `ClipFileLinks.css`: the look of ClipFileLinks.tsx
- `ClipFileLinks.tsx`: a clip's .fur to look at: opened in Furnace (a copy), downloaded, or as text
- `ClipOperations.css`: the look of ClipOperations.tsx
- `ClipOperations.tsx`: changes to a clip without Claude: an octave or a semitone up or down, Edit notes (the grid)
- `clip-peaks.ts`: a clip's waveform as peaks, from its samples
- `ControlGroup.css`: the look of ControlGroup.tsx
- `ControlGroup.tsx`: controls grouped under a very small label over a line
- `EditorBar.css`: the look of EditorBar.tsx
- `EditorBar.tsx`: the bar of controls above the timeline: the transport, then the groups
- `Fader.css`: the look of Fader.tsx
- `Fader.tsx`: a level's fader in decibels, standing in the mixer
- `grid.ts`: the snap grid: its steps, and a time snapped to one
- `lane-position.ts`: the track's lane under a point, and the tick a point is at, read from the page
- `SaveButtons.css`: the look of SaveButtons.tsx
- `SaveButtons.tsx`: Revert and Save, at the right of the song's title row
- `song-edits.ts`: the edits of the arrangement: move, trim, repeat, duplicate, remove a block; tracks
- `song-clips.ts`: the song's clips in the order it plays them, their blocks counted, each with its takes
- `SongClips.css`: the look of SongClips.tsx
- `SongClips.tsx`: the song's clips in a column right of the timeline, their takes under them, to select and drag onto a track
- `loop-range.ts`: the loop in bars: the whole song's, where a drag puts it, and in seconds
- `LoopLane.css`: the look of LoopLane.tsx
- `LoopLane.tsx`: the loop as a row under the ruler while looping: drag its edges, move it, draw one
- `Mixer.css`: the look of Mixer.tsx
- `Mixer.tsx`: the mixer, a row under the tracks that opens and folds from its head: a strip per track and the master (the player's volume, and mute)
- `MixerStrip.css`: the look of MixerStrip.tsx
- `MixerStrip.tsx`: a track's strip: its name, fader beside its meter, level, M and S
- `note-grid.ts`: the note grid's edits of a channel's rows: a note put in, moved, made longer or shorter, taken out; the pitches laid out
- `NoteGrid.css`: the look of NoteGrid.tsx
- `NoteGrid.tsx`: the note grid: one channel, time across and pitch up in a box that scrolls both ways, its notes edited and tried as a take
- `pending-takes.ts`: the takes on their way, a line each under the clip the clips column lists them with
- `MetronomeControls.css`: the look of MetronomeControls.tsx
- `MetronomeControls.tsx`: the metronome's switch and its volume slider, in the editor bar
- `structure-lane.ts`: where the structure a song was asked for lies on its timeline, from its start
- `StructureLane.css`: the look of StructureLane.tsx
- `StructureLane.tsx`: the structure a song was asked for, as a thin row of tiles above its first track
- `SongEditor.css`: the look of SongEditor.tsx
- `SelectionDetails.css`: the look of SelectionDetails.tsx
- `SelectionDetails.tsx`: the details of what is selected: a block's section and its clip's, a clip's, or a track's
- `SongEditor.tsx`: the editor: its bar of controls and the timeline's head kept in view, the timeline, the mixer, the song's clips, the details of what is selected
- `Timeline.css`: the look of Timeline.tsx and TimelineHead.tsx
- `timeline.ts`: where blocks lie in ticks, the bars shown, which bars are numbered
- `Timeline.tsx`: the tracks: a row per track with its blocks, the playhead
- `TimelineHead.tsx`: the timeline's head, kept in view: the ruler, the drag strip, the loop, the structure when shown, the playhead
- `TimelineBlock.css`: the look of TimelineBlock.tsx
- `TimelineBlock.tsx`: a block and its repeats on the timeline, dragged and trimmed
- `track-meter.ts`: how a track's level meter moves: up at once, eased down, its peak mark held
- `TrackMixButtons.css`: the look of TrackMixButtons.tsx
- `TrackMixButtons.tsx`: a track's M and S, in its row and in the mixer
- `TrackMeter.css`: the look of TrackMeter.tsx
- `TrackMeter.tsx`: a track's level meter, beside its name and in the mixer
- `tracks-window.ts`: how much of the tracks shows as the page scrolls past them: down to one row, the room kept under them
- `track-height.ts`: how tall the track rows are drawn: where they start, the slider's bounds
- `track-mix.ts`: which tracks are heard, by mute and solo
- `track-name-width.ts`: how wide the track names are drawn, and the bounds of dragging them
- `ToggleButton.css`: the look of ToggleButton.tsx
- `ToggleButton.tsx`: a switch in the editor bar: a button with a checkbox left of its label
- `TrackDetails.css`: the look of TrackDetails.tsx
- `TrackDetails.tsx`: the selected track's section: its name, remove
- `TransportButtons.css`: the look of TransportButtons.tsx
- `TransportButtons.tsx`: the transport, large: ■ stop (again: back to the start), ▶ / ❚❚ play or pause on one button, the time
- `use-block-drag.ts`: dragging blocks: moving and trimming them
- `use-clip-drag.ts`: dragging a clip onto a track's lane, as a new block
- `use-clip-prompt.ts`: the prompt on a clip kept, sent as takes, and the jobs of takes of it on their way
- `use-edge-scroll.ts`: the timeline scrolled while a drag is near its left or right edge
- `use-editor-keys.ts`: the editor's keys: Space, Delete, ⌘Z, ⇧⌘Z, ⌘S, Escape
- `use-element-width.ts`: an element's width, kept current
- `use-follow-playhead.ts`: the timeline keeping the playhead in the middle of the view while the song plays
- `use-heard-tracks.ts`: the player hearing only the tracks mute and solo leave, also while playing
- `use-loop.ts`: the editor's loop: on or off, the whole song until one is drawn
- `use-loop-drag.ts`: dragging in the loop row: an edge, the loop, or a new one drawn
- `use-metronome.ts`: the metronome clicking while the song plays, when it is on
- `use-note-grid-scroll.ts`: where the note grid's box is scrolled: to the notes at first, kept on them as the range grows
- `use-note-grid.ts`: the note grid's state: the notes read, the channel, the edits and their undo, Save as new take
- `use-playhead-line.ts`: the playhead moved every frame while the song plays
- `use-press-drag.ts`: a press that becomes a drag: a mouse at once, a finger after holding still
- `use-scroll-sync.ts`: the timeline's head and its tracks kept scrolled sideways to the same place
- `use-sticky-tracks.ts`: the tracks kept in view under the editor's head, shrinking to one row; a track brought into it
- `use-song-draft.ts`: the song being edited: a draft with undo and redo, the selection, save and revert
- `use-song-transport.ts`: playing the song from its cursor, stopping, moving the cursor
- `use-take-deletion.ts`: the take a block plays deleted, asked first, its clip's parent put in its blocks
- `use-take-swap.ts`: a take put in the selected block (and its clip's other blocks if asked), or in every block of the selected clip
- `use-timeline-pan.ts`: dragging empty space with a mouse scrolls the timeline
- `use-track-mix.ts`: the tracks muted and soloed while editing
- `use-track-name-resize.ts`: dragging the track names' edge resizes them, kept in the preferences
- `use-track-meter.ts`: a track's meter moved every frame while its song plays
- `use-wheel-zoom.ts`: scrolling over the timeline zooms it, about the moment under the pointer
- `use-zoom.ts`: the pixels to a bar: fitted, or zoomed in and out
- `versions.ts`: a clip's line of versions, oldest first
- `Waveform.css`: the look of Waveform.tsx
- `Waveform.tsx`: a slice of a clip's waveform on a canvas
